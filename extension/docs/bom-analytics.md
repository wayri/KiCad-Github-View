BOM / Stats is a read-only drawing-explorer tab in the schematic and PCB viewers. Counts follow the active assembly variant and can cover the active drawing or all loaded drawings. Multi-unit symbols share a saved file/reference identity; reused hierarchical instances are not expanded. PCB footprints keep separate UUID identities. Excluded-from-BOM parts remain in the summary but are omitted from grouped BOM rows and CSV export. Rows group value, footprint/library identifier and fitted/DNP state. Clicking a reference uses the ordinary source-bound selection/properties and paired-selection path. The BOM filter uses literal field queries (`ref:R1 value:10k`); Project search offers additional search modes.

Highlight DNP selects the current variant's unpopulated components on the active drawing, respects existing highlight style/layer visibility, and leaves source unchanged. It is disabled in 3D; the existing population overlay controls 3D assembly visibility. Clear highlights also clears this checkbox. A CSV export includes reference, value, footprint, population and file, quotes all fields, and prefixes potentially executable spreadsheet formulas with an apostrophe.

PCB statistics include footprint/pad counts, SMD and through-hole pads, drilled pads, vias, straight segments, arcs, saved thickness, straight and circular-arc centreline lengths, with totals by saved net and layer. Zone rows navigate by saved UUID; properties show outline and saved fill polygon area sums in mm². Layer sums refer only to saved zone filled polygons, not pads/tracks/all copper. Absolute straight-edge shoelace polygon areas are summed: overlaps count repeatedly and separate hole contours are not subtracted. This is not copper union, MCAD mass, actual board material area, clearance analysis or a zone refill. No saved fill is explicitly reported. Parsing is bounded to 8 MiB, the existing S-expression node/depth limits, and 200,000 polygon points. BOM computation is bounded to 100,000 objects; displayed rows are capped at 300 groups and 50 references per group.

Use **Board analytics** in the drawing toolbar to open the board metrics directly. Net-length rows select the saved net; zone rows select the saved zone UUID. Lengths sum individual saved tracks, including branches and repeated geometry, rather than calculating point-to-point signal paths. Via barrel lengths are not added. Malformed or unsupported tracks are omitted with a warning.

Placement review lists footprint origins at (0, 0), missing/invalid saved positions, and origins outside the supported straight-edge Edge.Cuts envelope. These are candidates for manual review, not a confirmed unplaced-component count. A board may legitimately use zero coordinates or have off-board parts, and an envelope does not prove containment within a concave outline. Curved or unsupported Edge.Cuts disable the envelope check rather than giving an incomplete bound.

Routing review shows candidate airwires and the number of nets with candidates, using the existing bounded saved-copper ratsnest analysis. Clicking a candidate net highlights it and shows airwires. Zones, arc copper and track-width overlap can cause false candidates; zero candidates does not certify routing completion. Imported DRC reports are shown individually with saved unconnected-finding and excluded-finding counts, date and source report path. Reports can be stale, incomplete by severity or ignored checks, and their association/revision is unverified. Findings are not summed across reports or called unrouted-net counts. Open saved DRC findings for the detailed evidence and location markers.

**Export board analytics JSON** retains repository, requested revision and active PCB path, saved geometry statistics, full computed candidate lists and routing limits, imported DRC provenance, and assembly counts with their selected variant and drawing scope. Geometry remains tied to the saved board and is not altered by assembly variants. UI lists show at most 300 net/placement/routing entries; exported arrays retain all results within parser and routing work limits. Android uses the native text-export bridge; the browser downloads the same report.

Track-arc parsing follows the [KiCad saved board format](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/file-formats/sexpr-pcb/#_track_arc). Numerical regression tests cover major and reversed arcs, translated coordinates, per-net/layer sums, invalid geometry, and placement uncertainty.
