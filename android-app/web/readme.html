Read Me

A guide to Pocket KiVi on phones and tablets. This guide is available offline.

Open a project

  • Use Open folder for a complete local project, including nested sheets and models.
  • Use Open files for a drawing or ZIP. Android’s picker can also open files from Drive and other storage providers.
  • For GitHub or GitLab, connect your account in Library and accounts, then open a repository. Private projects need permission from that provider.

Find your way

Use Project, Schematic and Layout to switch views. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom and Fit to see the drawing. Use the sheet tree or double-tap a subsheet to enter it. Back closes menus first, then returns to the parent sheet.

Search finds parts, nets and saved properties. Smart selection offers a choice when nearby objects overlap. Unselect clears the active selection; Clear highlights resets the highlights too.

Inspect and review

Tap a part to inspect its properties. Pull a panel’s handle up for more detail or down to close it. Hold a toolbar button for a tooltip; hold the drawing for contextual actions.

In Comments, write a title on the first line and the review below. You can @mention people and paste screenshots. Choose Comment to review the destination, then Post to publish. Save draft keeps the note locally.

Offline and temporary copies

Open Your projects to bookmark, star or save a project offline. Recently opened entries show their source, last-opened time and availability.

Large projects can be downloaded to private app storage. Temporary downloads expire after six hours; their history entries remain marked Expired. Use Clear temporary downloads to remove temporary files manually. Saved permanent copies remain.

Compare and present

Compare opens saved revisions for visual review. In Layout, switch to 3D to inspect the board and cross-select components. Presentation tools support a full-screen walkthrough and saved slides.

What to expect

Local viewing does not require an account. Online loading, thread updates and publishing need a connection. The viewer displays saved design data and supported saved analysis results; it does not replace native KiCad validation or run all external simulation tools.

Check the Privacy policy in Library and accounts for storage and account details.
