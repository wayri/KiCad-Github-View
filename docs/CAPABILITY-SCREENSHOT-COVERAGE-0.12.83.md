Fresh captures are catalogued separately in [the 0.12.83 gallery](SCREENSHOT-GALLERY-0.12.83.md).

**Key:** Retained = a relevant image exists, not necessarily every interaction. Requested = no known retained public screenshot identified here. Not visual proof = the screen may show controls, but cannot establish the outcome. Unsupported = documented host or feature limitation. “79” and “80” mean packaged 0.12.79 or 0.12.80 UI through a local public-source bridge, not an installed extension, device, or provider acceptance. “Preview” denotes a source or fixture preview; older screenshots retain their original version and limitations.
