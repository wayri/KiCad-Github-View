## Blocking gates and owner decisions

1. **UGC moderation:** source controls are implemented as described above, but the workflow currently hands reporting to an external email app and lacks a submission receipt. Verify whether this route meets Google's in-app reporting requirement; do not represent it as direct submission or proven compliance. Activate and evidence the [moderation procedure](MODERATION.md), including monitored reports and appropriate provider escalation. Google's policy includes specialized clients and requires ongoing moderation; UI controls alone and external provider moderation have not established that gate. [UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en).
2. **Publisher/contact/policy:** supplied contact and HTTPS policy deployment are prepared; confirm legal developer identity and Console policy entry; the owner confirmed mailbox monitoring. [User data requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en).
3. **Upload signing/enrollment:** upload key and signed AAB are prepared. Back up the key and recoverable password securely: local Windows DPAPI ciphertext is not a portable password backup. Enroll in Play App Signing, validate the uploaded AAB and install the Google-signed internal-track build. The existing sideloaded debug APK is not that build.
4. **Data Safety/deletion:** owner approves the worksheet and actual network/dependency inventory. Do not select “no data collected” solely because there is no analytics SDK. App-controlled WebViews/provider requests and explicit uploads count in the assessment. Reassess account deletion if app-account creation is added. [Data Safety worksheet](DATA-SAFETY.md), [account-deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en).
5. **Reviewer access:** provide valid reusable, location-independent English instructions and safe provider test access through Console, including authenticated features. Expiring/owner-assisted device codes alone are not reusable reviewer credentials. Never commit credentials. [Reviewer requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15748846?hl=en).
6. **Account/testing:** establish account type and creation date. Personal accounts created after November 13, 2023 require 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days, followed by production-access application. A new listing is not proof this rule applies or has been met. [Testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en).
7. **Store declarations/assets:** confirm category, countries, target audience, content rating, ads status and rights. Existing 512px icon and 1024×500 feature-graphic files are candidates, not approval. Supply genuine current phone/tablet screenshots without private designs or fabricated account state; check their metadata and rights. [Preview rules](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en).
8. **Runtime acceptance:** install through internal testing, verify minimum/current Android, phone/tablet, keyboard/Back/accessibility, large downloads, memory/disk failures, authentication, comments, 3D and offline reopening. Fix material crashes/ANRs and pre-launch findings before closed testing/production.

Follow the ordered [submission plan](SUBMISSION.md). Preserve final signed-artifact and test evidence against its exact version, not just this source audit date.
