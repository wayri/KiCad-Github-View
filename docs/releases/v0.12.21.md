# v0.12.21 — Auto-hide and pinnable side panes

Explorer, Properties and Comments now collapse to labelled tabs at the side of the drawing. Expand and Tall controls are replaced with Pin / Unpin and collapse arrows.

- Hover or click a side tab to reveal a full-height pane. Unpinned panes auto-hide after leaving, or when interacting with the drawing. Focused input and active resizing keep the pane open.
- Pin any pane or combination to keep it visible beside the drawing. Pinned Properties and Comments share the right dock vertically; width resizing remains available.
- Collapsing, auto-hiding, pinning and switching panes retain comments, drafts, hierarchy and selection state. Publication remains explicit.
- The drawing keeps at least 35% of available dock width when several panes are pinned. Transient panes preserve the full drawing beneath their overlay. Pin preferences last for the mounted viewer session.
