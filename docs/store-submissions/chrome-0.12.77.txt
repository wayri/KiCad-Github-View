WHAT'S NEW
- Fixed post-success errors and repeated destination prompts; faster visible-thread refresh with request caching and failure backoff.
- Durable point/area comment anchors, direct existing-conversation replies and native field/value properties.
- Simple file/tool project home, lazy settings preparation, restricted legacy preview and saved text variables.
- Direct assembly variant selection and browsable website commit history: load an exact revision or compare it with current.
- Visible cross-selection, Unsplit and theme controls; improved saved hierarchy and pin identities between 2D/3D and detached views.
- Movable net occurrence mini-viewports with rotation and loaded-sheet highlight indicators.
- Faster mechanical Draftsman through reused scenes and cached preview images.
- Experimental browser Fast PI: named nets, reviewed saved stackup, explicit inputs, validated copper contacts and DC voltage/current/density/loss overlays.
- Readable pin/pad numbers in 2D and 3D; distinct tool icons and milder curved hierarchy links.

LIMITS
History shows the website feed's initial window (up to 30 commits). Net quick views use loaded drawings. Fast PI is experimental fixed-temperature DC copper screening; no AC, return-network, thermal or qualification claims. Saved files and supported 3D assets are required. This independent viewer is not affiliated with KiCad, GitHub, GitLab or Mozilla.

PERMISSION AND DATA PRACTICES
No new permissions are introduced. Project/history reads use GitHub website/raw resources and bounded client-side buffering. Account and review actions remain explicit and can use provider APIs. Existing privacy policy and downloads/nativeMessaging justifications continue to apply.
